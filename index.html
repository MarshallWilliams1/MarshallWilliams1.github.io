About

Where analysis meets decision-making

I just graduated from Texas State University with a 3.90 GPA, Phi Beta Kappa, and a double major in Computer Science and History. My capstone research traced how French reparations policy and currency strategy drove the 1923 Ruhr Crisis, which is really just applied economic analysis: read the data, build the argument, explain what it means. CS gave me the tools to build the model, and History taught me how to make that argument hold up.

This past summer I interned at Planview, where I identified a costly business problem (escalated support cases) and built a forecasting system end to end, from stakeholder requirements through a production reporting pipeline delivered to leadership. Outside of that, I'm one of the team leads for H-E-B's roughly 100-person service team, managing shifts of up to 25 people at a time. I use the same instinct there: look at the trends, then change the process, to cut wait times and hit throughput targets. I even turned my League of Legends habit into a couple of side projects, from match analytics dashboards to a computer-vision tracker.

I'm currently looking for Financial Analyst roles where I can keep doing that: read the trend, build the model, make the call, then check the results and do it again.
